# Foundations quest catalog

Three daily quests and three weekly quests are available at a time: one combat, one mining and one harvesting goal. The next real time reset chooses a new set. Daily quests give 1,500 XP and 80 Coins. Weekly quests give 8,000 XP and 300 Coins.

Daily reset: 00:00 UTC. Weekly reset: Monday at 00:00 UTC.
